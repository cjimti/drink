Cachaça, stirred

Brazilian Negroni

1q,Q,Q,Ro

  • 1 1/4 oz Cachaça
  • 3/4 oz Campari
  • 3/4 oz Sweet vermouth

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Rocks, iced, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a quarter of cachaça against three-quarters each of Campari and sweet vermouth. The cane spirit leads a little; Campari is still a third of the drink. Stirred, rocks with ice, orange twist. A Negroni in sugarcane, grassy and bitter orange.

History

Swap gin for cachaça in a Negroni and the drink is a Brazilian Negroni, a substitution that spread with cachaça's craft revival in the 2000s. Difford's and later books print it as a Negroni ratio on cachaça. This menu pours an ounce and a quarter of cachaça against three-quarters each of Campari and sweet vermouth, over ice with an orange twist, so the cane leads the way the Boulevardier lets bourbon lead.
