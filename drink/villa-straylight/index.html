Gin, stirred

Villa Straylight

Barline 2,1,h,1b,c

  • 2 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1 barspoon Absinthe

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of gin to one of dry vermouth, the wet Martini on this menu, then a half ounce of orange liqueur as candied peel, and a barspoon of absinthe stirred in. The anise comes through over the orange. Stirred, coupe, no garnish. Wet gin, peel, and wormwood.

History

Villa Straylight does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals, nor under that name in Difford's, Punch, or Imbibe. It is a house drink on this menu, named for the Tessier-Ashpool villa at the end of Freeside in William Gibson's 1984 Neuromancer. The build is this menu's wet Martini, two ounces of gin to one of dry vermouth, plus a half ounce of orange liqueur and a barspoon of absinthe in the mix. Harry Johnson's 1888 Martini already carried orange curaçao; the absinthe is the Tuxedo's anise, poured rather than rinsed. Coupe, no garnish.
