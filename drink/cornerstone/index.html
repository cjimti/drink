Cachaça, stirred

Cornerstone

1h,h,q,2,co

  • 1 1/2 oz Cachaça
  • 1/2 oz Grenadine
  • 1/4 oz Fernet
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of cachaça, half an ounce of grenadine, a quarter of Fernet. The pomegranate is the sugar; the Fernet is mint and bitter against the cane. Two dashes of orange bitters are peel. Stirred, coupe, orange twist.

History

Brett Adams and Jacob Grier print the Cornerstone in Raising the Bar (2022), in the Fernet chapter: an ounce and a half of cachaça, half an ounce of grenadine, a quarter of Fernet, two dashes of orange bitters, orange twist. A drink of this name does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals. It drinks as an Old-Fashioned on cane spirit, grenadine in the sugar slot, Fernet as the bitter. This menu keeps that pour.
