Vodka Gimlet

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of vodka to three-quarters of lime and a half ounce of simple, the Gimlet's pour exactly. The lime is the flavor and the vodka is the cold and the length behind it. Shaken, coupe. Tart, thinly sweetened, short.

History

The Gimlet was gin and Rose's lime cordial, a navy drink that Harry MacElhone printed under the name in 1922 and that Raymond Chandler set loose in fiction, half gin and half Rose's, in The Long Goodbye in 1953. Vodka took the gin's slot as vodka took the American well; Difford's dates the substitution to the 1990s and the early 2000s and notes that gin has since taken the drink back. This menu pours the Gimlet's spec with the bottle changed: two ounces of vodka, three-quarters of fresh lime, a half ounce of simple, coupe.
