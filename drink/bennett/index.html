Gin, shaken

Bennett

1h,Q,h,2,c

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Lime juice
  • 1/2 oz Simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Anything with juice, egg, or syrup carrying the drink. Hard shake, double strain.

Coupe.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin against three-quarters of lime, a half ounce of simple, two dashes of Angostura. The bitters are bark and clove under the lime. Shaken, coupe. Lime, juniper, and a bitter finish. A Gimlet with spice, not honey.

History

Robert Vermeire prints the Bennett in 1922 Cocktails: How to Mix Them: two-sixths of a gill of Old Tom, one-sixth fresh lime, two dashes of Angostura, no sugar. "This cocktail, which is very popular in Chili, is called after the well-known and popular landowner and millionaire of that country." Nakata Seizo repeats it in Japan in 1926; Jimmy of Ciro's London does the same in 1930. The Chilean namesake is not further identified. This menu adds a half ounce of simple, pours an ounce and a half of gin and three-quarters of lime, two dashes of Angostura, coupe.
