Tequila, stirred

Rosita

Barline 1h,1,h,h,1,Ro

  • 1 1/2 oz Tequila
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1/2 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Rocks, iced, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of tequila against a full ounce of Campari, so the bitter leads. Half an ounce each of sweet and dry vermouth split the wine, a Perfect Negroni with agave. One dash of Angostura is spice. Stirred, rocks, ice, orange twist.

History

The Rosita first appears, creator unknown, in the 1974 Mr. Boston Official Bartender's Guide: one ounce tequila, one ounce Campari, half an ounce each of dry and sweet vermouth, stirred on cracked ice, lemon twist. Gary Regan reprinted it in The Bartender's Bible (1991) with more tequila and a dash of Angostura, then forgot he had. Terry Sullivan and Regan traced it back in 2007 in the San Francisco Chronicle. Mexico City is sometimes named; no period citation supports that. This menu keeps Regan's ounce and a half of tequila and the dash of Angostura, yet it pours a full ounce of Campari and garnishes with orange.
