Gin, stirred

Physically Forgotten

1h,Q,h,2,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Cynar
  • 1/2 oz Maraschino liqueur
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters of Cynar, half an ounce of maraschino. The gin leads; artichoke bitter and cherry liqueur split the rest. Two dashes of orange bitters. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist.

History

Alise Moffatt built Physically Forgotten at Shift Drinks in Portland, Oregon. Brett Adams and Jacob Grier print it in Raising the Bar (2022): an ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters Cynar, half an ounce of maraschino, two dashes of orange bitters, lemon twist. It drinks as a Martinez with Cynar in the vermouth slot. This menu keeps that pour.
