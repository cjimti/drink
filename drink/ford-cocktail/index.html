Gin, stirred

Ford Cocktail

Barline 1h,1h,q,2,co

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 1 1/2 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Bénédictine
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Equal ounces and a half of gin and dry vermouth, then a quarter ounce of Bénédictine, which puts honey and herbs through the middle of it. Two dashes of orange bitters keep peel in the mix. Stirred, coupe, orange twist. A wet Martini with an herbal film.

History

George J. Kappeler prints the Ford Cocktail in 1895 Modern American Drinks: three dashes of Bénédictine, three dashes of orange bitters, half a jigger of Tom gin, half a jigger of French vermouth, orange peel. He does not name a Ford. Ted Haigh, in Vintage Spirits and Forgotten Cocktails, points to Malcolm Webster Ford, the 1880s all-around athlete and journalist, rather than Henry, whose company postdates the recipe. This menu keeps the equal gin and dry vermouth and the orange bitters, yet it pours a quarter ounce of Bénédictine, coupe, orange.
