Taste

Two ounces of sweet vermouth, then tonic to the top of an iced highball. Wormwood, vanilla, and dried orange from the vermouth over quinine, with the wine's sugar and the tonic's meeting in the middle. Low proof and long; an orange twist on top.

History

Spain drinks vermouth as its own occasion, la hora del vermut, poured from the tap in bars across Madrid and Barcelona from the late nineteenth century on and served over ice with a slice of orange, an olive, and a splash of soda or tonic. Punch lists tonic with vermouth beside sherry and white port as the Iberian answer to the Gin and Tonic, fortified wine in the spirit's place. This menu pours two ounces of sweet vermouth and tops with tonic over ice, orange twist.
