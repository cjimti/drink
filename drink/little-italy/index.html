Little Italy

2,Q,h,cc

  • 2 oz Rye

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of rye to three-quarters of sweet vermouth, so the whiskey leads. Half an ounce of Cynar is artichoke bitter in the Campari slot. Stirred, coupe, cherry. A rye Negroni, leaner, no gin.

History

Audrey Saunders built the Little Italy at Pegu Club in New York in 2005: two ounces of rye, three-quarters sweet vermouth, half an ounce of Cynar, cherry. It is a Manhattan with Cynar in place of bitters, or a rye Negroni with the gin dropped. Robert Simonson treats it as a modern classic. This menu keeps Saunders's pour and the cherry.
