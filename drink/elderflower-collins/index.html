Elderflower Collins

Barline 1h,Q,Q,t,Hl

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Elderflower liqueur
  • 3/4 oz Lemon juice
  • top with Soda water

Build in the glass over ice, then lift once with a barspoon.

Highball, iced, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters of elderflower liqueur, three-quarters of lemon, and soda over ice. The elderflower is the sugar in this Collins, so the sweetness arrives floral, and the lemon keeps it tart underneath. Long and light, lemon twist.

History

A Collins is gin, lemon, sugar, and soda, and the modern habit is to move the sugar into a liqueur. St-Germain, the first elderflower liqueur, arrived in 2007, made from flowers picked in the Haute-Savoie and macerated within 48 hours, and the Elderflower Collins followed the bottle onto back bars. Difford's prints a version that adds a touch of maraschino beside the elderflower. No creator and no date are attached to it. This menu pours an ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters of elderflower, three-quarters of lemon, and soda over ice, with a lemon twist.
