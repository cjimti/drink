Aged Rum, stirred

Pedro Martinez

Barline 2,1,q,3,10,2b,R

  • 2 oz Aged rum
  • 1 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Maraschino liqueur
  • 3 dashes Orange bitters
  • 10 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 2 barspoons Absinthe

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Rocks, iced.

Open in the menu

Taste

Aged rum and sweet vermouth sit two to one, the Martinez ratio, with a quarter ounce of maraschino as the cherry glue. Ten dashes of Angostura is a bark flood, and three orange bitters keep peel in the mix. Two barspoons of absinthe is a pour you can taste, anise laid over the rum. Ice in the rocks glass is the point: this one is meant to sit and dilute.

History

The Pedro Martinez is a rum Martinez from Michael Shea at Rum Club in Portland, Oregon, published by Imbibe in August 2020. Shea builds it on Demerara rum with Cocchi Vermouth di Torino, maraschino, aromatic bitters, orange bitters, and a few drops of absinthe, stirred and strained over a large cube. It is a modern sugarcane version of the gin Martinez, not a recipe from the nineteenth-century manuals. This menu keeps the two-to-one rum and vermouth, the quarter of maraschino, and the rocks glass with ice, yet it pours ten dashes of Angostura and two barspoons of absinthe, heavier than Shea's drops.
