Gin, stirred

Wiffin

1h,Q,h,1d,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Cognac
  • 1 dash Absinthe

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin against three-quarters of sweet vermouth, then a half ounce of cognac, grape spirit under gin. One dash of absinthe is a thin anise wash, not a pour. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist. Two spirits and vermouth, wormwood at the edge.

History

The Wiffin is not a pre-1970 bar-book drink. P. G. Wodehouse, in the 1928 story "The Passing of Ambrose," has Ambrose Wiffin sketch a cocktail of gin, Italian vermouth, a spot of old brandy, and a dash of absinthe. David Wondrich reconstructed the formula in Australian Bartender in 2011: English gin, Italian vermouth, cognac, and a dash or two of absinthe. This menu pours an ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters sweet vermouth, a half ounce of cognac, and one dash of absinthe, coupe, lemon.
