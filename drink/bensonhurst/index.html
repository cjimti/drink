Rye, stirred

Bensonhurst

Barline 2,1,h,b,c

  • 2 oz Rye
  • 1 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Maraschino liqueur
  • 1 barspoon Cynar

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of rye, a full ounce of dry vermouth, half an ounce of maraschino, a barspoon of Cynar. The dry vermouth keeps the whiskey lean, and the maraschino carries the sugar as cherry pit and almond. The barspoon of Cynar is the artichoke bitterness that arrives last and stays. Stirred, coupe, no ice.

History

Chad Solomon made the Bensonhurst in 2006 while working at Milk & Honey in Manhattan, and named it for the Brooklyn neighbourhood near where he lived. It is a riff on the Brooklyn, which is itself a Manhattan built on dry vermouth and maraschino, with Cynar taking the fourth slot. Dozens of Brooklyn variations came out of New York in the years after, and this is one of the few still ordered by name. This menu pours two ounces of rye, an ounce of dry vermouth, half an ounce of maraschino, and a barspoon of Cynar, stirred, coupe.
