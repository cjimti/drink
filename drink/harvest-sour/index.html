Taste

Equal ounces of rye and apple brandy. Three-quarters of an ounce of lemon, half an ounce of simple, egg white. The foam is the texture; grated cinnamon on the coupe is the spice. Orchard, pepper, lemon, and a dry foam.

History

The Harvest Sour is Sam Ross at Attaboy in New York, printed by Punch and dated by Difford's to around 2017: one ounce rye, one ounce applejack, three-quarters lemon, three-quarters simple, egg white, apple slice and cinnamon. Ross's other split-base drinks, the Paper Plane among them, are the parent method. A drink of this name does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals. This menu keeps the equal rye and apple brandy, the lemon, the egg white, and the cinnamon. It pours half an ounce of simple rather than three-quarters, and it skips the apple slice.
