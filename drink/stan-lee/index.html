Light Rum, shaken

Stan Lee

1h,Q,Q,h,1,c

  • 1 1/2 oz Light rum
  • 3/4 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Lime juice
  • 1/2 oz Grenadine
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Coupe.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of light rum and three-quarters gin, so the rum leads and the gin is botanicals underneath. Three-quarters lime, half an ounce of grenadine, one dash of Angostura. Pink, bark through the pomegranate. Shaken, coupe.

History

A drink called Stan Lee does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals, nor under that name in Difford's, Punch, or Imbibe. The nearest parent is Harry Craddock's Stanley Cocktail in the 1930 Savoy Cocktail Book: equal gin and rum, a sixth each of lemon juice and grenadine. This menu keeps the split base and the grenadine, swaps lime for lemon, leads with an ounce and a half of rum against three-quarters gin, and adds a dash of Angostura.
