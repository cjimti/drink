The Rum Drink

Anything with juice, egg, or syrup carrying the drink. Hard shake, double strain.

Open in the menu

Taste

The Daiquiri's two ounces of light rum and three-quarters lime, with only a half ounce of simple, then a barspoon of Campari. The Campari is a bitter orange film, not a pour. Pink at the rim, dry at the back. Coupe.

History

John Lermayer mixed The Rum Drink at Sweet Liberty in Miami Beach; Eater printed it in March 2016: two ounces of white rum, three-quarters lime, three-quarters simple, one spoon of Campari, shaken, coupe. Kevin Armstrong's Dry Daiquiri, at London's Match Bar in 2005, is an earlier Campari Daiquiri, with passion fruit syrup in the mix. This menu keeps Lermayer's name, rum, lime, and barspoon of Campari, and cuts the simple to half an ounce.
