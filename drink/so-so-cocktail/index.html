Apple Brandy, stirred

So So Cocktail

1,1,h,h,cc

  • 1 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Apple brandy
  • 1/2 oz Grenadine

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe, cherry.

Open in the menu

Taste

Equal gin and dry vermouth, a half ounce of apple brandy riding underneath, and a half ounce of grenadine that tastes of pomegranate. The gin is the drink; the apple is a peel of orchard. Dry vermouth keeps it lean, so the grenadine is the only roundness. Coupe, cherry.

History

The So So Cocktail appears in Harry MacElhone's 1923 Harry of Ciro's ABC of Mixing Cocktails, credited to Mr. P. Soso, manager of Ciro's Club in London (MacElhone also names the Kit-Kat Club). The printed formula is one-sixth grenadine, one-sixth Calvados, one-third Cinzano vermouth, one-third gin. Harry Craddock repeats it in the 1930 Savoy Cocktail Book with Italian vermouth in place of the brand. This menu files under apple brandy and is mostly gin. It also swaps the Italian vermouth for dry, and it pours a half ounce of grenadine rather than a sixth.

  • Difford's Guide, So So Cocktail
  • The Savoy Cocktail Book (1930), p. 151
