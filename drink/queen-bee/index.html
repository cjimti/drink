Open in the menu

Taste

Three-quarters each of vodka, elderflower, and lemon, a quarter of honey, an ounce of Champagne. The Champagne is the volume. Shaken, then the wine, coupe, lemon twist. Flower, honey, bubbles, a small drink that foams from the fizz.

History

Charles H. Baker prints a Queen Bee in 1951 The South American Gentleman's Companion on apple brandy, lemon, honey, and curaçao; that is not this drink. A vodka, elderflower, lemon, honey, and Champagne Queen Bee does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals. St-Germain, the elderflower liqueur this recipe calls for, launched in 2007. This menu pours three-quarters each of vodka, elderflower, and lemon, a quarter of honey syrup, and an ounce of Champagne, coupe, lemon twist.
