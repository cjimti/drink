Gin, stirred

Racquet Club

2,1,1b,2,cl

  • 2 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1 barspoon Crème de cacao
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of gin to one of dry vermouth, a dry Martini ratio. One barspoon of crème de cacao is chocolate as a film. Two dashes of orange bitters are peel. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist. Gin, dry wine, and a thin cocoa finish.

History

Hugo R. Ensslin prints a Racquet Club Cocktail in Recipes for Mixed Drinks (1916/1917): gin, French vermouth, orange bitters. Harry Craddock's Savoy keeps gin and French vermouth with orange bitters and a lemon. Later specs put a barspoon of crème de cacao in the dressing, chocolate against the dry gin. This menu pours two ounces of gin, one of dry vermouth, a barspoon of cacao, two dashes of orange bitters, lemon twist.
