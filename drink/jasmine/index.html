Gin, shaken

Jasmine

Barline 1h,Q,q,q,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Lemon juice
  • 1/4 oz Campari
  • 1/4 oz Orange liqueur

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin against three-quarters of lemon; a quarter each of Campari and orange liqueur. The Campari is bitter orange in a sour, so the drink reads pink and like grapefruit. Shaken, coupe, lemon twist. Gin, pith, and a thin bitter film.

History

Paul Harrington mixed the first Jasmine in 1993 at Townhouse Bar and Grill in Emeryville, California, for Matt Jasmin, a classmate who asked for something Harrington had never made. Harrington riffed on a Pegu Club he knew from Trader Vic's: Campari in place of the two bitters, lemon in place of lime, and a lemon twist. He misspelled Jasmin's name and the drink kept the extra e. It prints in Cocktail: The Drinks Bible for the 21st Century, which Harrington wrote with Laura Moorhead in 1998. This menu is Harrington's pour: an ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters lemon, a quarter each of Campari and orange liqueur, coupe, lemon twist.
