Scotch, shaken

Mickie Walker

1h,h,h,h,cc

  • 1 1/2 oz Scotch
  • 1/2 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Grenadine
  • 1/2 oz Lemon juice

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Coupe, cherry.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of scotch, then a half ounce each of sweet vermouth, grenadine, and lemon. The lemon and pomegranate are real pours, not dashes, so the drink is shaken and pink. Coupe, cherry. Scotch, grape, and a sour pomegranate cut.

History

Harry Craddock prints the Mickie Walker Cocktail in The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930: one dash grenadine, one dash lemon juice, a quarter Italian vermouth, three-quarters Scotch whisky, shaken. The spelling is Craddock's; the name tracks Edward Patrick "Mickey" Walker, the New Jersey boxer who took the welterweight title in 1922 and the middleweight in 1926. The 1937 UKBG Approved Cocktails repeats Craddock's dashes. This menu keeps the name Mickie Walker and the four ingredients, yet it pours a half ounce each of vermouth, grenadine, and lemon against an ounce and a half of scotch.

  • The Savoy Cocktail Book (1930), p. 104
