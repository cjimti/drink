Bourbon, stirred

Fancy Cocktail

1h,q,1b,2,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Bourbon
  • 1/4 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1 barspoon Demerara syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of bourbon, a quarter ounce of orange liqueur, one barspoon of demerara, two dashes of Angostura. The orange liqueur is peel and a little sugar. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist. An Old-Fashioned with curacao in the sugar slot, served up.

History

Jerry Thomas prints the Fancy Brandy Cocktail and Fancy Gin Cocktail in the 1862 Bar-Tender's Guide as the plain Cocktail strained into a fancy wine-glass, lemon on the rim and a piece of peel thrown on top. Wayne Curtis, in Imbibe, notes that the difference is mostly the glass. Thomas's later Improved Cocktail is the one that adds maraschino and absinthe; the version here is the older fancy, whiskey with a splash of orange liqueur rather than those. This menu pours an ounce and a half of bourbon, a quarter of orange liqueur, a barspoon of demerara, two dashes of Angostura, coupe, lemon.
