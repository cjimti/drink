Bourbon, shaken

Whiskey Daisy

Barline 2,Q,q,1b,R

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 3/4 oz Lemon juice
  • 1/4 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1 barspoon Simple syrup

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Rocks, iced.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters lemon, a quarter of orange liqueur, one barspoon of simple. The orange liqueur is peel in the sugar slot. Shaken, rocks with ice. A short sour that sits.

History

Jerry Thomas prints the Daisy in the 1876 Bar-Tender's Guide as a sour sweetened with orange liqueur and topped with seltzer, in brandy, whiskey, gin, and rum versions. David Wondrich, in The Oxford Companion, notes an 1883 claim that Billy Taylor gave the idea to Frank Haas at Fred Eberlin's bar near the New York Stock Exchange about 1873. Later daisies swapped grenadine for curaçao and went long. This menu keeps the whiskey, lemon, and orange liqueur, adds a barspoon of simple, and pours it over ice with no soda.
