Taste

Equal ounces of Campari, crème de cacao, and cream. No base spirit. Bitter orange and chocolate under cream. Shaken, coupe. Pink, cocoa, and a bitter finish.

History

Brett Adams and Jacob Grier print the Campari Alexander in Raising the Bar (2022): equal Campari, crème de cacao, and cream, shaken, coupe. It is Ensslin's Alexander with Campari in the gin slot. A drink of this name does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals. This menu keeps the equal ounces.

  • Willamette Week, Raising the Bar
