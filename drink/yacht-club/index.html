Light Rum, stirred

Yacht Club

Barline 2,1,q,c

  • 2 oz Light rum
  • 1 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Apricot liqueur

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of light rum to one of sweet vermouth, a Manhattan ratio on cane. A quarter ounce of apricot liqueur carries stone fruit through it. Stirred, coupe, no twist on the menu. Rum first, then grape, then a thin apricot film.

History

A Yacht Club Cocktail of rum, Italian vermouth, and apricot prints in the 1948 Ron Daiquiri Coctelera booklet from Havana: two-thirds Daiquiri rum, one-third Italian vermouth, a dash of apricot brandy, stirred. David Embury includes a Yacht Club of the same bones in The Fine Art of Mixing Drinks the same year; Difford's adapts that printing. This menu keeps the two-to-one rum and sweet vermouth and puts a quarter ounce of apricot liqueur in place of the dash.
