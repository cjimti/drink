Vermouth, stirred

Tip Top

2,Q,2,cl

  • 2 oz Dry vermouth
  • 3/4 oz Bénédictine
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of dry vermouth, three-quarters Benedictine, two dashes of orange bitters. The liqueur is a real pour of honey and spice, not a few dashes. Orange bitters pull peel through the wine. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist.

History

Albert Stevens Crockett prints Tiptop in Old Waldorf Bar Days (1931): four dashes of Benedictine, two dashes of Angostura, one jigger of French vermouth, stirred, lemon peel. No bartender is named. Difford's later scales the Benedictine to three-quarters of an ounce. This menu keeps that pour and the lemon twist, and uses two dashes of orange bitters in place of Angostura.

  • Difford's Guide, Tip Top Cocktail
  • Old Waldorf Bar Days (1931), p. 167
