Stingless Bee

2,Q,h,h,c3

  • 2 oz Tequila
  • 3/4 oz Honey syrup
  • 1/2 oz Lime juice

Coupe, 3 dashes of bitters.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of tequila, three-quarters honey syrup, half lime, half lemon. The citrus is split, so neither fruit wins; the honey is waxier than sugar. Egg white gives it a foam cap. Three dashes of Peychaud's are dropped on the foam, cherry-anise on the white. Coupe.

History

The Bee's Knees is a gin sour with honey, first printed in Paris around 1929. Frank Meier's Bar de Ritz recipe appears in Cocktails de Paris that year; a Brooklyn Standard Union article of 22 April 1929 credits Mrs. J.J. Brown of Denver and Paris, later called Molly Brown. Both claims stay in print. A drink called Stingless Bee does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals, nor under that name in Difford's, Punch, or Imbibe. This menu swaps gin for tequila, splits the citrus between lime and lemon, shakes egg white, and drops three dashes of Peychaud's on the foam.
