Gin, stirred

Quill

1,1,1,q,co

  • 1 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Absinthe

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Equal ounces of gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth, so Campari is a third of the drink, then a quarter ounce of absinthe in the mix, not a rinse. That is a real pour of anise, enough to sit on the Campari rather than perfume the glass. Stirred, coupe, orange twist. Bitter orange, grape, and wormwood, served up.

History

The Quill first prints in the 1996 edition of Harry's ABC of Mixing Cocktails, where Andrew and Duncan MacElhone added forty-odd recipes to Harry MacElhone's original books and credited this one to Frank C. Payne of New York. It is not in the earlier editions, nor in MacElhone's 1927 Barflies and Cocktails. Payne was a theatrical press agent in the Theatrical Press Representatives of America, which published a magazine called The Quill. The drink is a Negroni with absinthe. This menu keeps the equal ounces and puts a quarter ounce of absinthe in the build, coupe, orange twist.
