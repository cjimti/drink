Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters orange liqueur, half an ounce each of sweet vermouth and lemon. The orange liqueur is heavier than the lemon, so peel leads. Shaken, coupe, lemon twist. A Manhattan sour: grape, whiskey, and candy citrus.

History

The drink is named for the thoroughbred who won 20 of 21 races in 1919 and 1920, including the Preakness and Belmont. Simon Difford finds no mid-century book recipe and treats the cocktail as modern; a 1915 newspaper Man-o'-war cocktail is condensed milk, alcohol, and eggs. The Indianapolis Star printed the bourbon, Cointreau, sweet vermouth, and lemon formula in 2015 as a Derby drink. This menu pours two ounces of bourbon, three-quarters orange liqueur, half each of sweet vermouth and lemon, coupe, lemon twist.
