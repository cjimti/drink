Apple Brandy, stirred

Corpse Reviver No. 1

1h,Q,Q,cc

  • 1 1/2 oz Cognac
  • 3/4 oz Apple brandy
  • 3/4 oz Sweet vermouth

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, cherry.

Open in the menu

Taste

Cognac first, an ounce and a half, then three-quarters each of apple brandy and sweet vermouth. The apple is a second brandy. No juice, no bitters, stirred. Two brandies and vermouth, orchard under grape. Coupe, cherry.

History

Harry Craddock prints Corpse Reviver No. 1 in The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930: half brandy, a quarter apple brandy or Calvados, a quarter Italian vermouth, "to be taken before 11 a.m., or whenever steam and energy are needed." Difford's Guide notes that the drink is sometimes credited to Frank Meier at the Ritz Bar in Paris, although Meier makes no claim in his 1936 The Artistry of Mixing Drinks. The No. 2, gin, Lillet, orange liqueur, lemon, and absinthe, is a different cocktail and is not on this menu. This one leads with cognac. The menu pours an ounce and a half of cognac and three-quarters each of apple brandy and sweet vermouth.
