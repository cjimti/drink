Vodka Martini

  • 2 oz Vodka

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of vodka, half an ounce of dry vermouth. Four to one; the wine is in the drink. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist. Cold, grape.

History

A vodka Martini prints in a 1935 Smirnoff pamphlet at three parts vodka to one of French vermouth, with lemon peel in the mixing glass. Lucius Beebe puts two versions in The Stork Club Bar Book in 1946. Ted Saucier, in 1952 Bottoms Up, credits photographer Jerome Zerbe with a four-to-one Vodkatini and a lemon twist. John G. Martin at Heublein spent the 1940s and 1950s selling Smirnoff as a mixer. This menu is two ounces of vodka to half an ounce of dry vermouth, stirred, coupe, lemon.
