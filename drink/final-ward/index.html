Taste

Equal three-quarters of rye, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lemon. The Last Word on whiskey: herbal, cherry, and lemon against rye pepper. Shaken, coupe.

History

Phil Ward built the Final Ward at Death & Co in New York in 2007, a Last Word with rye in place of gin and lemon in place of lime. Robert Simonson treats it as a modern classic. Equal parts, shaken, coupe, no garnish. This menu keeps Ward's pour.
