Gin, shaken

Fine and Dandy

1h,Q,Q,1b,2,c

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 3/4 oz Orange liqueur
  • 3/4 oz Lemon juice
  • 1 barspoon Simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Coupe.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin, three-quarters orange liqueur, three-quarters lemon, a barspoon of simple. Two dashes of Angostura pull bark and clove through the orange. Shaken, coupe. Peel, pith, and a little clove on the gin.

History

The Fine and Dandy prints in Harry Craddock's 1930 Savoy Cocktail Book: a quarter lemon, a quarter Cointreau, half Plymouth gin, and one dash of Angostura, shaken. It is a White Lady with bitters and without egg, under another name. Craddock does not claim it. This menu keeps the two-to-one gin against equal orange liqueur and lemon, adds a barspoon of simple, and doubles the bitters to two dashes.
