Fernet and Ginger

1,4,RLw

  • 1 oz Fernet

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Open in the menu

Taste

One ounce of Fernet, four ounces of ginger beer, over ice. The ginger beer is the drink; the Fernet is mint and bitter through the spice. Lime squeezed in and dropped. A highball. It sits.

History

Fernet and ginger beer is a bartender's highball, Fernet-Branca lengthened with ginger, a squeeze of lime. It does not appear under a fixed name in the pre-1970 manuals; it belongs to the Fernet-Branca cult that took hold in San Francisco bars in the 2000s and then everywhere else. Brett Adams and Jacob Grier print it in Raising the Bar (2022) as an ounce of Fernet and four of ginger beer, lime wedge in. This menu keeps that pour, rocks with ice.
