Taste

Two ounces of cognac, two of cream, half an ounce of simple, shaken hard and poured over ice in a rocks glass. Cold, rich, and only a little sweet; the cream rounds the brandy and the grape and oak come through it. Grated cinnamon on top is the first thing you taste. The classic grate is nutmeg, and this shelf keeps cinnamon.

History

Milk punch is colonial: spirit, milk, sugar, and spice, drunk in Britain and America through the eighteenth century. New Orleans kept it as a morning drink, and Brennan's made the brandy version a fixture of the city's brunch, shaken with half-and-half, sugar, and vanilla and dusted with nutmeg. Difford's pours it with cognac and whole milk. This menu pours two ounces of cognac, two of cream, and half an ounce of simple, shaken, over ice, grated cinnamon.
