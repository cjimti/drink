Rye, stirred

Improved Coctail

Barline 2,1b,1b,1b,2,Rl

  • 2 oz Rye
  • 1 barspoon Maraschino liqueur
  • 1 barspoon Absinthe
  • 1 barspoon Simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Rocks, iced, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two ounces of rye, then a barspoon each of maraschino, absinthe, and simple. The absinthe is in the drink, anise against cherry pit and sugar. Two dashes of Angostura. Rocks with ice, lemon. Rye first, then wormwood.

History

Jerry Thomas prints the Improved Whiskey Cocktail in the 1876 Bar-Tender's Guide as the Improved Brandy Cocktail with whiskey in place of brandy: Boker's or Angostura, gum, maraschino, a dash of absinthe, lemon oil, shaken. The improvement is European liqueur on the old cocktail of spirit, sugar, and bitters, the same dressing drinkers later asked to skip when they called for an old-fashioned whiskey cocktail. The name here spells it Coctail, as the paper menu does. This menu pours two ounces of rye, a barspoon each of maraschino, absinthe, and simple, two dashes of Angostura, over ice with a lemon twist.
