Rye, stirred

The Slope

2h,Q,q,1,cc

  • 2 1/2 oz Rye
  • 3/4 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Apricot liqueur
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, cherry.

Open in the menu

Taste

Two and a half ounces of rye, three-quarters sweet vermouth, a quarter of apricot liqueur. Stone fruit against a large rye pour. One dash of Angostura. Stirred, coupe, cherry. The apricot is orchard on a Manhattan.

History

Julie Reiner built The Slope in 2006 and named it for Park Slope, first served at Flatiron Lounge, then as the house Manhattan at Clover Club, which she opened in Brooklyn in 2008. Jim Meehan printed the rye, Punt e Mes, apricot, and Angostura formula in GQ in 2010. This menu keeps the two and a half ounces of rye, three-quarters vermouth, and a quarter of apricot, yet it pours sweet vermouth in place of Punt e Mes.
