Hanky Panky

1h,1h,1b,co

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 1 1/2 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1 barspoon Fernet

Spirit only. Stir on ice until cold, strain, no dilution past that.

Coupe, orange twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

Equal ounces and a half of gin and sweet vermouth, a fifty-fifty Martinez ratio. One barspoon of Fernet is mint and bitter, enough to taste, not enough to own the drink. Stirred, coupe, orange twist.

History

Ada Coleman created the Hanky Panky at the Savoy's American Bar in London, where she was head bartender from 1903 to 1926. Harry Craddock prints it in the 1930 Savoy Cocktail Book, credited to her: equal Italian vermouth and gin, two dashes of Fernet-Branca, orange peel. The story attached is actor Charles Hawtrey asking her for something with a punch, and naming the result. This menu keeps the equal gin and sweet vermouth, pours a barspoon of Fernet, coupe, orange.
