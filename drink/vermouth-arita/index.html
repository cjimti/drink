Tequila, shaken

Vermouth-arita

Barline 1h,1,h,h,Rc

  • 1 1/2 oz Tequila
  • 1 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1/2 oz Lime juice

Shake hard with ice, then strain.

Rocks, iced, cherry.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of tequila against a full ounce of sweet vermouth, so the wine is a second base. Half an ounce of orange liqueur and half of lime are the Margarita's other two, cut down. Shaken, rocks, ice, cherry. A tequila Manhattan that took a lime.

History

A drink called Vermouth-arita does not appear in the pre-1970 manuals, nor under that name in Difford's, Punch, or Imbibe. The parent is the Margarita: tequila, orange liqueur, and lime, a formula in print by 1937 as William J. Tarling's Picador and under this name by 1953. This menu keeps the tequila and the shake, puts a full ounce of sweet vermouth in the glass, cuts the orange liqueur and lime to half an ounce each, and pours it over ice with a cherry.
