Gin, stirred

Journalist

1h,h,h,q,q,1,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Gin
  • 1/2 oz Sweet vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1/4 oz Lemon juice
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of gin, then a half ounce each of sweet and dry vermouth, a Perfect Martini ratio. A quarter ounce of orange liqueur and a quarter ounce of lemon sit in the same glass: peel and pith, enough lemon that the drink is tart even stirred. One dash of Angostura. Coupe, lemon twist. Citrus in a stirred gin drink.

History

Harry Craddock prints the Journalist in The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930: two-thirds Gordon's dry gin, one-sixth each of French and Italian vermouth, two dashes of lemon juice, two dashes of curaçao, one dash of Angostura, shaken. No bartender is named. Paul Clarke, in Serious Eats, treats it as a Perfect Martini with dashes of lemon and orange liqueur. This menu keeps both vermouths and the Angostura, yet it pours a quarter ounce each of orange liqueur and lemon, stirred, coupe, lemon.
