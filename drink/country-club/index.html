Light Rum, stirred

Country Club

Barline 1h,1,h,1,cl

  • 1 1/2 oz Light rum
  • 1 oz Dry vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Orange liqueur
  • 1 dash Orange bitters

Stir with ice until cold, then strain.

Coupe, lemon twist.

Open in the menu

Taste

An ounce and a half of light rum against a full ounce of dry vermouth, nearly even. Half an ounce of orange liqueur is peel and sugar, and one dash of orange bitters sits under both. Stirred, coupe, lemon twist. Lean rum, dry grape, candied orange.

History

Albert Stevens Crockett prints the Country Club in the 1935 Old Waldorf-Astoria Bar Book as half Bacardi, half French vermouth, and a dash of orange curaçao, among the amendments to the old Waldorf list. William T. Boothby's 1934 World Drinks has a different Country Club, whiskey with orange bitters in a bar glass. The Savoy Country Club Cooler is French vermouth, grenadine, and soda, a different drink. This menu keeps Crockett's light rum, dry vermouth, and orange liqueur, yet it pours an ounce and a half of rum, a full ounce of vermouth, a half ounce of orange liqueur, and a dash of orange bitters, coupe, lemon.
